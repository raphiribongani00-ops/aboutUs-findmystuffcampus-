Find My Stuff Campus is a platform that helps students find their belongings that they lost at Campus. The developer lost a bank card and only realised after 2 weeks that he had lost it. He didn't know where to start looking at. Whilst on a quest to find his bank card he saw many bank cards, id cards, driver's licences at safekeeping that people had lost and didn't come to collect. Maybe they don't even know that what they had lost has been found. He saw a great need to solve a problem of lost and found items using a realtime digital platform.
